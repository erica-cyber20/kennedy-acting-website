About Me

A bright storyteller with big-stage dreams

Biography

Kennedy is a young actress and performer who loves stepping into new characters and sharing stories with heart. In a parent-approved voice, this space can introduce her personality, training, favorite kinds of roles, and the joy she brings to every rehearsal, classroom, audition, and performance.

She enjoys acting, storytelling, performing, learning scripts, practicing scenes, and discovering how a character thinks, feels, and moves.

Personality Confident, kind, curious, expressive
Hobbies Dance, singing, reading, soccer, improv games
Favorite Roles Funny friend, brave leader, imaginative dreamer
Goals Book commercials, films, theater, and voiceover work
